# Built & Broken — Channel Kit

Example channel for AI YouTube Studio. Niche: business stories, covering how
companies were built and how they broke.

## Name

**Built & Broken**

Handle: `@BuiltAndBrokenTV`. Fallbacks: `@BuiltNBroken`, `@BuiltAndBrokenStories`.

## Channel description (paste into YouTube Studio → Customization → Basic info)

```
Every empire has two stories: how it was built, and how it broke.

Built & Broken tells the true stories behind the world's biggest companies.
We cover the bets that made billionaires, the mistakes that erased giants,
and the small decisions that changed everything. Nokia, Blockbuster, IKEA,
Kodak, Nintendo, and the brands you use every day.

New story every weekday.

Our videos are researched and scripted with AI tools, fact-checked and
approved by a human before they go out, and narrated with synthetic voice.
Sources are listed in every description.

Built with AI YouTube Studio (open source): https://github.com/dennis299/ai-youtube-studio
```

## Keywords (Studio → Settings → Channel → Keywords)

```
business stories, company history, rise and fall, business documentary, why companies fail, startup stories, brand history, entrepreneurship, corporate history, business case study
```

## Content pillars

1. **The Fall**: giants that collapsed (Nokia, Blockbuster, Kodak, BlackBerry, Toys "R" Us).
2. **The Build**: origin stories of brands people use daily (IKEA, Nintendo, Red Bull, Zara).
3. **The Bet**: one decision that made or broke a company (Netflix killing DVDs, Apple's iPhone gamble).
4. **The Comeback**: companies that broke and rebuilt (Lego, Marvel, Apple 1997).
